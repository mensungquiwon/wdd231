Royal Forex

1. Purpose of the Website

The purpose of the Royal Forex website is to simulate a financial tracking system that provides micro-loan tracking, displays daily foreign exchange rates in Liberia, and monitors the sale of scratch cards.

2. Target Audience

The target audience includes small business owners, individuals seeking small community loans, money exchange users, and scratch card vendors in Liberia.

3. Scenarios

Scenario 1: Micro-Loan Applicant

Question: I am a small business owner who needs a small loan to support my business. How can Royal Forex help me track a micro-loan?

Answer: The Loan Tracking page allows users to submit loan requests and view their current loan status, helping them understand how loans are monitored within the system.

Scenario 2: Forex Rate Checker

Question: I want to know the current exchange rate between the US Dollar and the Liberian Dollar. Where can I find this information?

Answer: The Forex section displays daily exchange rates fetched from a public forex API, providing users with up-to-date currency information.
