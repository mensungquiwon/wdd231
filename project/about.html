About Royal Forex

Royal Forex is a comprehensive financial tracking system designed to empower individuals and businesses in Liberia. Our platform offers three core features:

  • Micro-Loan Tracking: Apply for micro-loans, track your loan status, and manage repayments all in one place.
  • Daily Forex Rates: Stay informed with up-to-date exchange rates for the Liberian Dollar (LRD) against major currencies.
  • Scratch Card Sales Monitoring: Keep track of scratch card sales and inventory for local retailers.

Our mission is to provide accessible financial tools that support economic growth and financial literacy in Liberia. Whether you're a small business owner seeking a loan or an individual looking to stay informed about forex rates, Royal Forex is here to help you succeed.
